## About the Company

Acme Robotics was founded in 2018 and builds warehouse automation robots.
The company headquarters is in Toronto, Canada, with a second engineering
office in Berlin, Germany. As of 2025, Acme employs roughly 240 people.

## Products

Acme sells three main products:

- **PickBot** — an autonomous mobile robot that picks items from shelves.
  It has a battery life of 9 hours and a maximum payload of 15 kilograms.
- **SortHub** — a stationary sorting station that processes up to 1,200
  packages per hour.
- **FleetOS** — the cloud software platform that coordinates all robots in a
  warehouse and provides a real-time dashboard.

## Support Policy

Standard support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time.
Premium customers receive 24/7 support. The standard response time for a
critical issue is 2 hours; for non-critical issues it is 1 business day.

Warranty on all hardware products is 24 months from the date of purchase.
Software subscriptions are billed annually and renew automatically unless
cancelled at least 30 days before the renewal date.

## Vacation Policy

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year, plus public
holidays. Vacation days do not roll over to the next year unless approved by
a manager. New employees accrue vacation from their first day but cannot take
paid leave during the first 90 days of employment.

## Security

All employees must use two-factor authentication on company accounts.
Customer data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. Acme is SOC 2 Type II
compliant and undergoes an external security audit every year.
