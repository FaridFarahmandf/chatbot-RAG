## Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)

The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) is a shared film universe produced by
Marvel Studios. It began in 2008 with Iron Man. Kevin Feige is the president
of Marvel Studios and the producer of the MCU films. The first three phases
of the MCU are together called the Infinity Saga, which ends with Avengers:
Endgame and Spider-Man: Far From Home in 2019.

### Iron Man (2008)

Iron Man was released in 2008 and is the first film in the Marvel Cinematic
Universe. It was directed by Jon Favreau. Robert Downey Jr. plays Tony Stark,
a billionaire weapons maker who builds a powered suit of armor after being
kidnapped. Gwyneth Paltrow plays Pepper Potts and Jeff Bridges plays the
villain Obadiah Stane. Iron Man earned about $585 million worldwide. Its
post-credits scene introduces Nick Fury, played by Samuel L. Jackson.

### The Avengers (2012)

The Avengers was released in 2012 and was written and directed by Joss
Whedon. It is the first film to bring together Iron Man, Captain America,
Thor, Hulk, Black Widow and Hawkeye as a team. The villain of The Avengers is
Loki, played by Tom Hiddleston, who leads an alien army called the Chitauri
in an attack on New York City. The Avengers earned about $1.52 billion
worldwide.

### Guardians of the Galaxy (2014)

Guardians of the Galaxy was released in 2014 and was directed by James Gunn.
Chris Pratt plays Peter Quill, also known as Star-Lord. The team also includes
Gamora (Zoe Saldaña), Drax (Dave Bautista), Rocket (voiced by Bradley Cooper)
and Groot (voiced by Vin Diesel). The villain is Ronan the Accuser. Guardians
of the Galaxy earned about $773 million worldwide and is known for its 1970s
pop soundtrack, called "Awesome Mix Vol. 1".

### Captain America: Civil War (2016)

Captain America: Civil War was released in 2016 and was directed by Anthony
and Joe Russo. The Avengers split into two sides over the Sokovia Accords, a
law that puts superheroes under government control. Tony Stark (Iron Man)
supports the Accords and Steve Rogers (Captain America, played by Chris
Evans) opposes them. The film introduces Tom Holland as Spider-Man and
Chadwick Boseman as Black Panther. It earned about $1.15 billion worldwide.

### Black Panther (2018)

Black Panther was released in 2018 and was directed by Ryan Coogler. Chadwick
Boseman plays T'Challa, the king of the African nation of Wakanda. Michael B.
Jordan plays the villain Erik Killmonger. Black Panther earned about $1.35
billion worldwide. It was the first superhero film nominated for the Academy
Award for Best Picture, and it won three Oscars: Best Original Score, Best
Costume Design and Best Production Design.

### Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

Avengers: Infinity War was released in 2018 and was directed by Anthony and
Joe Russo. The villain is Thanos, played by Josh Brolin, who wants to collect
all six Infinity Stones. The six Infinity Stones are the Space, Mind, Reality,
Power, Time and Soul stones. At the end of the film Thanos snaps his fingers
and wipes out half of all life in the universe. Avengers: Infinity War earned
about $2.05 billion worldwide.

### Avengers: Endgame (2019)

Avengers: Endgame was released in 2019 and was directed by Anthony and Joe
Russo. It is the direct sequel to Avengers: Infinity War. The surviving
Avengers travel back in time to collect the Infinity Stones and undo the snap
by Thanos. Tony Stark sacrifices his life to defeat Thanos, and Steve Rogers
passes the Captain America shield to Sam Wilson. Avengers: Endgame has a
running time of 181 minutes and earned about $2.8 billion worldwide, making
it the highest-grossing Marvel film.

### Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

Spider-Man: No Way Home was released in 2021 and was directed by Jon Watts.
Tom Holland plays Peter Parker. A spell cast by Doctor Strange goes wrong and
opens the multiverse, bringing in villains from earlier Spider-Man films,
including Green Goblin (Willem Dafoe) and Doctor Octopus (Alfred Molina).
Tobey Maguire and Andrew Garfield return as their own versions of Spider-Man.
The film was co-produced by Sony Pictures and Marvel Studios and earned about
$1.9 billion worldwide.

## DC Films

DC films are based on DC Comics characters and are distributed by Warner
Bros. From 2013 to 2023 most DC films belonged to a shared universe called
the DC Extended Universe (DCEU), which started with Man of Steel. In 2022
James Gunn and Peter Safran became co-heads of DC Studios and launched a new
shared universe called the DC Universe (DCU).

### Man of Steel (2013)

Man of Steel was released in 2013 and was directed by Zack Snyder. Henry
Cavill plays Clark Kent, also known as Superman. Amy Adams plays Lois Lane
and Michael Shannon plays the villain General Zod. Man of Steel is the first
film in the DC Extended Universe and earned about $668 million worldwide. The
music was composed by Hans Zimmer.

### Wonder Woman (2017)

Wonder Woman was released in 2017 and was directed by Patty Jenkins. Gal
Gadot plays Diana, an Amazon princess from the island of Themyscira. Chris
Pine plays Steve Trevor, an American pilot. The story takes place during
World War I, and the villain is Ares, the god of war. Wonder Woman earned
about $822 million worldwide.

### Aquaman (2018)

Aquaman was released in 2018 and was directed by James Wan. Jason Momoa plays
Arthur Curry, the half-human heir to the underwater kingdom of Atlantis.
Amber Heard plays Mera, and Patrick Wilson plays Orm, Arthur's half-brother
and the main villain. Aquaman earned about $1.15 billion worldwide, making it
the highest-grossing DC film.

### Joker (2019)

Joker was released in 2019 and was directed by Todd Phillips. Joaquin Phoenix
plays Arthur Fleck, a failed comedian in Gotham City who becomes the Joker.
Joker is a standalone film and is not part of the DC Extended Universe. It
earned about $1.07 billion worldwide and was the first R-rated film to pass
$1 billion. Joker received 11 Academy Award nominations and won two: Best
Actor for Joaquin Phoenix and Best Original Score for Hildur Guðnadóttir.

### Superman (2025)

Superman was released in 2025 and was written and directed by James Gunn. It
is the first film of the new DC Universe (DCU). David Corenswet plays
Superman, Rachel Brosnahan plays Lois Lane and Nicholas Hoult plays the
villain Lex Luthor. Superman's dog Krypto also appears in the film. Superman
earned about $615 million worldwide.

## Batman Films

Batman is a DC Comics character created by Bob Kane and Bill Finger in 1939.
Batman is the secret identity of Bruce Wayne, a billionaire from Gotham City
who fights crime after his parents were murdered. Batman has no superpowers.
Several actors have played Batman in live-action films, including Michael
Keaton, Val Kilmer, George Clooney, Christian Bale, Ben Affleck and Robert
Pattinson.

### Batman (1989)

Batman was released in 1989 and was directed by Tim Burton. Michael Keaton
plays Bruce Wayne / Batman and Jack Nicholson plays the Joker. Kim Basinger
plays the photojournalist Vicki Vale. The score was composed by Danny Elfman.
Batman earned about $411 million worldwide. Tim Burton and Michael Keaton
returned for the sequel, Batman Returns, in 1992.

### Batman Begins (2005)

Batman Begins was released in 2005 and was directed by Christopher Nolan. It
is the first film in The Dark Knight Trilogy. Christian Bale plays Bruce
Wayne / Batman. The film tells Batman's origin story, including his training
with the League of Shadows. The villains are Ra's al Ghul, played by Liam
Neeson, and Scarecrow, played by Cillian Murphy. Michael Caine plays Alfred
and Gary Oldman plays James Gordon. Batman Begins earned about $373 million
worldwide.

### The Dark Knight (2008)

The Dark Knight was released in 2008 and was directed by Christopher Nolan.
It is the second film in The Dark Knight Trilogy. Christian Bale plays Batman
and Heath Ledger plays the Joker. Aaron Eckhart plays Harvey Dent, the
district attorney who becomes Two-Face. Heath Ledger won the Academy Award
for Best Supporting Actor for his role as the Joker; the award was given
after his death in January 2008. The Dark Knight has a running time of 152
minutes and earned about $1 billion worldwide.

### The Dark Knight Rises (2012)

The Dark Knight Rises was released in 2012 and was directed by Christopher
Nolan. It is the third and final film in The Dark Knight Trilogy. The story
takes place eight years after The Dark Knight. The villain is Bane, played by
Tom Hardy, who takes control of Gotham City. Anne Hathaway plays Selina Kyle
(Catwoman) and Joseph Gordon-Levitt plays police officer John Blake. The Dark
Knight Rises earned about $1.08 billion worldwide, the most of any Batman
film.

### The Batman (2022)

The Batman was released in 2022 and was directed by Matt Reeves. Robert
Pattinson plays Bruce Wayne in his second year as Batman. The villain is the
Riddler, played by Paul Dano, a serial killer who targets Gotham's corrupt
officials. Zoë Kravitz plays Selina Kyle (Catwoman), Colin Farrell plays the
Penguin and Jeffrey Wright plays James Gordon. The Batman has a running time
of 176 minutes and earned about $772 million worldwide. It is separate from
the DC Extended Universe and led to the HBO series The Penguin in 2024.
