# Manchester United

Manchester United is one of the most famous and widely supported football clubs in the world. Based in Old Trafford, Greater Manchester, England, the club plays in the Premier League and is known by the nickname **"The Red Devils."**

## History

### Early Years
The club began in 1878 as Newton Heath, a works team for the Lancashire and Yorkshire Railway. After near bankruptcy, it was rescued in 1902 and renamed Manchester United. The club won its first league title in 1908 and moved to Old Trafford in 1910.

### The Busby Era (1945 to 1969)
Sir Matt Busby rebuilt the club after the Second World War and trusted young players developed by the club, a group known as the **"Busby Babes."** On 6 February 1958, the team's plane crashed in Munich on the way home from a European Cup match. Twenty-three people died, including eight players. Busby survived and rebuilt again, and in 1968 United became the first English club to win the European Cup, with a team featuring Bobby Charlton, Denis Law and George Best.

### The Ferguson Era (1986 to 2013)
Sir Alex Ferguson became the most successful manager in British football history. Under him, United won 13 Premier League titles, 5 FA Cups and 2 Champions Leagues. The highlight was the **1999 Treble** (Premier League, FA Cup and Champions League), sealed by two stoppage-time goals against Bayern Munich in Barcelona. Famous players from this period include Eric Cantona, Roy Keane, Ryan Giggs, Paul Scholes, David Beckham, Wayne Rooney and Cristiano Ronaldo.

### After Ferguson (2013 to 2025)
Since Ferguson retired, the club has struggled to find stability, going through David Moyes, Louis van Gaal, José Mourinho, Ole Gunnar Solskjær, Ralf Rangnick, Erik ten Hag and Ruben Amorim. There were still trophies, including the Europa League (2017), FA Cups (2016, 2024) and League Cups (2017, 2023). The 2024-25 season, however, ended in 15th place in the league, one of the worst finishes in the club's modern history.

### The Carrick Turnaround (2026)
Ruben Amorim was dismissed in January 2026, and former United midfielder Michael Carrick took over as interim head coach. He transformed results, winning 10 and drawing 3 of his 15 league games, beating Arsenal, Manchester City, Liverpool and Chelsea along the way. United finished **3rd** in the 2025-26 Premier League, returning to the Champions League, and Carrick was appointed permanent head coach in May 2026.

## Current Situation (October 2026)

The 2026-27 season has started slowly. United sit around 12th with five points from their first five league games, and the squad is dealing with several injuries. During the October international break, the team played a behind-closed-doors friendly against INEOS-owned Lausanne-Sport, winning 2-1, which also saw Matthijs de Ligt and Carlos Baleba return from injury. The next league match is at home against Tottenham on 10 October.

## Club Legends

- **Sir Bobby Charlton**: Munich survivor and 1966 World Cup winner, a symbol of the club for decades
- **George Best**: Ballon d'Or winner in 1968, one of the most gifted players of his generation
- **Eric Cantona**: The catalyst for the 1990s dominance
- **Ryan Giggs**: The club's record appearance maker
- **Wayne Rooney**: United's all-time top goalscorer
- **Cristiano Ronaldo**: Won his first Ballon d'Or at United in 2008
- **Bruno Fernandes**: Club captain and the creative leader of the current team

## Culture and Identity

- **Youth tradition**: United have had a youth academy graduate in every matchday squad since the 1930s, a record of more than 4,000 consecutive games.
- **Global fanbase**: The club claims hundreds of millions of supporters worldwide and is one of the most valuable sports brands on the planet.
- **The Munich memorial**: Every February the club remembers the victims of the 1958 air disaster, a central part of its identity.
- **The "United Way"**: Attacking, entertaining football and faith in young players are seen as the club's core values.
